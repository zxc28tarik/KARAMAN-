## Part 1: Production art direction (current)

### 1.1 Target
- **Look:** *The Last Faith*-style gothic, soulslike pixel art, with *Castlevania: SotN* 4:3 framing and character-to-room proportions. Mood: 90s–2000s dark anime, moderately dark, not pitch black. Cultural setting: Ottoman and Central-European frontier dark fantasy (Ottoman architecture, Janissaries, kilij and turbans next to Gothic churches and alpine fortresses).
- **What we copy from the references is method, not assets.** The Last Faith pixelates detailed paintings or pre-renders, limits the palette per area, and adds 2D light and fog. We do the same in code with `PixelPipeline`. Never import or trace reference screenshots.
- **What it must not look like:** generic mobile fantasy, flat vector, clean cartoon outlines, over-saturated rainbow FX, smooth HD sprites over a pixel background (mixels), or noisy "AI texture soup" with no readable clusters.

### 1.2 Rendering model (fixed)
World rendered at 4× → quantised to 720×540 game pixels → zone palette (12-tone main ramp + 25 accent colours) → displayed at 2× (1080 lines) with sub-pixel scroll and vignette; the HUD is crisp on its own layer. Details and numbers: `ASSET_CONTRACTS.md` §1 and §6.

### 1.3 Value and colour structure (per shot)
| Plane | Value | Colour | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sky / far vista | mid-dark, low contrast, lightest near the horizon haze | zone main ramp only, desaturated | large flat clusters (Kuwahara-pixelised); no small high-contrast detail |
| Mid architecture | darker than the vista, more contrast | main ramp, rare warm window or fire accents | silhouettes first |
| Playfield (walkable layer + props) | mid values; top edge of walkable surfaces readable as a light line | main ramp + wood / fire accents | the most texture detail in the scene |
| **Actors** | must separate from the playfield: a light rim on the key side plus a dark outline against light areas. Targets in VISUAL_ACCEPTANCE O5a (silhouette) and O5b (interior must not be a black cut-out) | skin, crimson, brass and steel accents allowed | most contrast and colour in the frame |
| Foreground silhouettes | near-black (ramp 0–1) | none | fast parallax, never covering the action line |

Zone keys (`PixelLook.For`): **Kızıl Surlar** rose-violet sunset · **Yanmış Mahalle** charcoal and ash with distant fire · **Kurt Geçidi** moonlit blue-green · **Karanlık Kale** snow, pale lavender-grey. Each zone is near-monochrome; colour accents are rare and therefore meaningful (fire, blood, banners, gold).

### 1.4 Characters
- **Production method (locked by the user, 2026-10-11):** single-piece, frame-by-frame pixel sprites generated by `Tools/pixel_characters.py` and rendered whole by `PixelCharacter`. No part or cut-out rigs. The painted rigid-part rig was retired from the game (code kept in `_yedek/retired-paintedrig-20261011/`).
- Height ≈ 62 game px (idle). Proportions are heroic but grounded, about 7 heads tall; the head must read as turban + face + beard.
- Construction: 5-tone material ramps (outline, shadow, mid, light, highlight). A 1-game-px outline that is selective: darkest ramp on the outer silhouette, softened toward the lit side. Material patterns (lamellar, mail, cloth folds) are clusters of at least 2 px, never single-pixel noise.
- Identity colours. **Karamani:** ivory-and-red turban, bronze lamellar + mail, burgundy/crimson cloth, steel kilij. **Janissary guards:** ivory hood, weathered mail, bronze, burgundy. Captains are about 9% larger.
- Each character keeps one recognisable colour block, such as Karamani's crimson cape, so it reads at small size.
- Known issue (2026-10-08 hero sheet): the face reads as a dark blob under a pale turban mass, so no facial planes or beard are readable. Fix before integration.

### 1.5 Animation style
- Frame-by-frame look. Key poses must be strong enough to read as a still. Use few frames with strong spacing rather than many frames with even spacing.
- Attacks follow anticipation (2–3 frames), then a smear or active frame, then a 1–2 frame impact hold, then follow-through and recovery. Hit-stop, impact flash and particles are timed together.
- The cape and turban tail are secondary motion that lags 1–2 frames. Idle breathes (6 frames).
- Locomotion frame advance is driven by distance walked (stride), so feet never skate.

### 1.6 Environment
- Modular: tile and slab pieces cut flat on their sides, with the walkable top as a perfectly horizontal line that matches physics within 1 game px.
- Environmental storytelling per zone: banners, braziers, ruins, wolf statues, snow. Props sit on the ground (contact shadow or occlusion line), never floating.
- Detail density is highest on the playfield, lower on mid architecture and lowest on the vista.

### 1.7 VFX and post
- Every particle is pixel-snapped and 1–2 game px (snow, ash, embers), or a palette-limited sprite. No soft alpha gradients that turn into banding after quantisation, unless dithered on purpose.
- Hit FX: 1–2 frame white or warm flash plus a 1-px slash line plus a few sparks, coloured from the fire ramp. Blood uses the crimson ramp, sparingly.
- Light: warm point lights (braziers, lanterns) against cool ambient. Light shafts and fog bands stay subtle and below the actors' contrast.

### 1.8 Approval
The art-director approves the per-slice visual spec (`Docs/specs/<slice>-visual-spec.md`) at Gate A, and signs Gate E together with the visual-reviewer. Only the user can change the locks in DESIGN_CONSTRAINTS.md.

## Kemerli sur: tam üretim yönergesi

Dosya: `rampart.png`

> Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: a production-ready transparent foreground environment sprite for a high-fidelity 2.5D side-scrolling Ottoman dark-fantasy Unity game, matching the attached richly painted stone fortress references. Primary request: ONE continuous wide horizontal medieval masonry rampart/bridge, with three deep arched openings beneath a flat walkable top. This is a NEW isolated game asset, not a full screenshot. True RGBA transparent background; all three arched openings must be transparent too, showing whatever background Unity places behind. Composition: wide landscape 2.4:1 silhouette, entire rampart visible with slight alpha padding; top walkable edge perfectly horizontal near the upper 8 percent, left and right ends cut vertically flat for modular tiling, no protruding battlements or objects on top. The top 20 percent is a substantial solid masonry deck. Below it are three broad Romanesque rounded arches down to the lower edge, with substantial stone piers between them. Structure reaches down to the canvas bottom; no ground under it. Materials: individually irregular large dark weathered limestone blocks, deep mortar crevices, cracked ornate arch keystones, dark iron ties, subtle wet sheen, sparse hanging roots, moss and chipped rubble edges. Authentic detailed stone masonry, hand-painted photorealistic dark fantasy quality, fine surface grain, confident readable silhouette. Lighting: neutral cold overcast key with a gentle warm side rim; no huge baked orange fire glow. Orthographic camera facing the vertical facade perfectly straight, no perspective, no top-down tilt, no isometric angle, no depth extrusion at either end. Avoid: sky, opaque backdrop, scenery inside openings, people, flames, torches, crates, text, HUD, labels, grid, checkerboard, ground shadow, white outline, repeated identical brick rows, clean cartoon edges, stretched textures. This piece must look like richly detailed realistic architecture when layered under separately animated painted characters.
